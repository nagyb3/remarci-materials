SEA/EAK exam information

Dates

The primary exam date is: December 15 (Monday).

The secondary (retake/improvement) exam date is: January 22 (Thursday).

In both cases, the exam begins at 17:00 in the Main Computer Room (Lovarda, South Building 0-308).

  • Arrive 15 minutes before start.
  • There will be many other students; come near the entrance, as you will be seated first.

Conditions

The exam begins with theory related questions about the topics covered in the semester. There, you may not use any help at all (e.g. handouts, code, Internet).

After a short break, the exam continues with the practice part. There, you may use helping materials and even search the Internet. However, “active” help (e.g. AI, chatting, exchanging files) is forbidden.

  • You must disable all such support (e.g. AI assistants) in your editor.
  • Visiting pages or opening tools whose main purpose is to allow such communication will fail the exam, so don’t do it.

During the lab part, you will be using your own laptops.

  • Make sure beforehand that your installation and the sample codes work right.
